CAREWELL FICTIONAL HEALTH INSURANCE CO. LTD.
Policy Schedule and Key Terms - Family Shield Basic Plan

Policyholder: E. Familyhead (fictional)
Policy period: 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2028

1. Sum insured: Rs. 5,00,000 per policy year, on a family floater basis.

2. Premium: The annual premium is Rs. 14,500 including GST.

3. Initial waiting period: Claims for any illness in the first 30 days from the policy start date are not payable. Accidents are covered from day one.

4. Pre-existing diseases: Any pre-existing disease is covered only after 36 months of continuous coverage.

5. Specific diseases: Treatment for cataract, hernia, joint replacement and kidney stones is covered only after a waiting period of 24 months.

6. Co-payment: The insured shall bear 20% of every admissible claim amount.

7. Room rent: Room rent is limited to 1% of the sum insured per day. If a higher room is chosen, all related expenses will be paid in the same proportion.

8. Sub-limits: Cataract surgery is payable up to Rs. 40,000 per eye. Knee replacement is payable up to Rs. 1,50,000 per knee.

9. Permanent exclusions: The policy does not cover cosmetic or plastic surgery, treatment for obesity, self-inflicted injuries, and injuries due to war or nuclear risks.

10. Cashless: Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals.

11. Claim intimation: The insurer must be informed within 24 hours of an emergency hospitalisation, and at least 48 hours before a planned hospitalisation.

12. Claim process: For cashless claims, show the health card at the network hospital desk, which sends the pre-authorisation form to us. For reimbursement, submit the claim form with original bills, discharge summary and reports within 30 days of discharge.
